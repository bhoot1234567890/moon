I'm a software engineer who works across the entire stack — from writing VPU firmware that adds suspend-to-RAM to a Raspberry Pi, to building cycle-accurate emulators in C++20, to deploying ML models on AWS and shipping 3D web experiences with Three.js.

Most engineers specialize in one layer. My differentiator is full-vertical mastery: I'm equally comfortable at the bare-metal register level and at the polished-frontend level.
